Scan a paper

Upload a photo or scan of a newspaper page or any paper. You get a credibility report with its signals and limits, never a bare "fake or real".

Privacy: when claim checking is switched on, the recognised text is sent to Groq to find claims, and those claims are sent to Google's Fact Check Tools. With it off, nothing leaves this machine.

        How it works
        
        
        The page is uploaded, the text is read with OCR, and the document is registered. An AI model
        then picks out checkable claims, and each claim is searched for in published fact-checks. Only those
        fact-checks count as evidence: the AI never decides what is true. Most claims have never been
        fact-checked, so "not enough evidence" is a common and honest answer. OCR can misread skewed or
        multi-column pages, and fact-check matches are keyword-based, so read them yourself.
